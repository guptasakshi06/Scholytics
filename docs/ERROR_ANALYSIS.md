# S10 measured error analysis

The frozen test shows that full C preprocessing increased OCR errors relative to original-image A. Replacing keywords with the frozen learned classifier improved classification of the observed unsupported examples on the same A OCR, without improving field extraction. These are results on a small synthetic dataset, not evidence of real applicant accuracy or scholarship eligibility.

## Main measured findings

Across the same 14,900 reference characters / 2,092 words, A/B/C have **3/22/50 character edits** and **4/22/46 word edits**, respectively. Their corpus CER is 0.0201% / 0.1477% / 0.3356%; WER is 0.1912% / 1.0516% / 2.1989%. No page failed processing or produced an empty OCR prediction in any route; none were excluded. Full text includes the printed demonstration labels, headers and footers. These small absolute error totals should accompany percentages.

All keyword routes have six-class macro-F1 0.944444; A learned has 1.000000. Keywords accept all 44 supported-type pages and four of eight `other` pages; four unsupported pages abstain and remain false negatives. Their titles include `FEE RECEIPT` and `UNSUPPORTED NOTICE`, outside the literal baseline's recognized title vocabulary. Learned classification accepts all eight observed unsupported examples. The [confusion figure](verification/s10/assets/classification_A.png) and [per-class table](verification/s10/assets/classification_per_class.csv) retain support (income 12, each other label eight), precision, recall and unresolved outputs. Model probabilities remain uncalibrated; this is a limited unsupported-content comparison.

Exact normalized field value **plus unit plus person role** matches are A 161/164, B 153/164 and C 158/164. A learned also has 161/164. Four erased income amounts have unreadable references and are counted separately, rather than changing the present-target denominator. A has two missing/invalid outputs and one wrong nonnull output; B has nine and two; C has six and zero. True-type extraction and predicted-type extraction have the same counts in every route because these classification abstentions concern documents without supported field targets. This does not remove the need to report both routes: their [separate per-field accounting](verification/s10/assets/fields_per_type.csv) covers all five supported types. No reference `absent` field occurs in this final set; absence handling remains a development regression.

A keyword, A learned and C each match 24/24 application headlines, 120/120 item states and 24/24 completeness values. B matches 22/24, 117/120 and 22/24. Its clean applicant-17 income amount is read correctly as text but rejected under the frozen token-score heuristic, so two reference-ready bundles become `needs_clearer_upload`. A third item-state disagreement occurs under a reference `needs_review` headline. This shows that text correctness, confidence heuristics and readiness are different measures.

Every route has confirmed-missing TP=4, FP=0, FN=0, TN=116, hence missing precision/recall/F1=1.0. All 120 items remain in accounting, including review/unreadable/not-applicable states. This binary metric misses false unreadable/review outcomes: `C_no_filter` matches only 20/24 headlines and 115/120 item states, while `C_kernel_2x2` matches 18/24 and 106/120, with completeness matching only 16/24. Full outcome and requirement-state confusions are in each [metric report](verification/s10/assets/run/summary.json).

**False ready = 0/16 reference non-ready bundles in every route.** Reference support is ready eight, incomplete four, needs-clearer-upload four and needs-review eight. Predicted-ready counts are eight for A/C, six for B/no-filter and four for kernel 2x2; false-ready share among predicted-ready is also zero with these separate denominators. The predeclared zero-denominator convention is null / not estimable, although this test's denominators are nonzero. No population false-ready bound or significance claim follows from four applicant groups and correlated scenarios.

## What the ablations establish here

The [all-variant plot](verification/s10/assets/ocr_ablations.png) and generated table retain every declared result, including unfavorable ones. Character edits are A 3, B 22, C 50, no-deskew 37, no-CLAHE 165, no-filter 35, no-threshold-or-morphology 0, no-morphology 50, gray-only 3, kernel 1x2 83, kernel 2x1 111 and kernel 2x2 158. Kernel dimensions are height by width, with black text and the recorded open-before-close convention.

Removing thresholding and its dependent morphology leaves grayscale, deskew, CLAHE and median filtering. That predeclared diagnostic has zero OCR edits and 164/164 present fields here. It was not eligible in S09's bounded kernel selection, so it does not replace frozen C or become a new confirmed winner. Any revised method needs separate development selection and a new confirmatory holdout. No per-page branch oracle is used.

Identity 1x1 morphology and removing morphology produce identical semantic outputs. Nonidentity kernels increase errors; removing CLAHE produces the highest edit total, even though its field count improves relative to C. Removing filtering reduces character edits but increases word edits and harms bundle outcomes. Deskew removal helps this set in aggregate. These interactions preclude treating any one stage as universally beneficial or inferring downstream accuracy from CER alone.

## Inspected success and failure cases

- `exp-14-0-clean`: A/B/C preserve the printed `79.25`, classify marksheet and have zero OCR edits. The [success panel](verification/s10/assets/success.png) demonstrates agreement rather than a fabricated improvement.
- `exp-16-5-rotation`: A has zero character edits, C four. The [paired page](verification/s10/assets/C_largest_change.png) and [seven-stage sequence](verification/s10/assets/C_stages.png) show deskew and binarization effects on the receipt's small print. Both keyword predictions abstain. The paired comparison associates errors with the full processing chain; it does not isolate a single causal stage for each character.
- `exp-14-1-rotation`: A extracts annual income 183914 INR/year; C returns null with a low token-score reason, although the number remains visible in the [mapped processed-coordinate crop](verification/s10/assets/field_detail_C.png). Kernel 2x2 recovers this one value while worsening aggregate results. This is an illustration, not permission to select a method per page.
- `exp-14-0-low_contrast`: A has zero edits and kernel 2x2 has 12. The [full panel](verification/s10/assets/kernel_largest_change.png) looks darker after processing, but academic-year confidence drops below the heuristic ([crop](verification/s10/assets/field_detail_C_kernel_2x2.png)); `804` becomes a valid-looking extracted `604`, and the percentage label/value fails. Strokes and punctuation visibly change. A clear appearance and a format-valid number do not guarantee correctness.
- `exp-15-2-rotation`: A and B extract course `BSC Chemistry` for printed/reference `BSc Chemistry`. The frozen exact string normalization counts this as one wrong nonnull field; it is retained rather than case-normalized after seeing the test. C cannot resolve the course label. This optional-field error does not change the configured checklist outcome.
- `exp-14-1-no_amount`: the [erased amount panel](verification/s10/assets/erased_amount.png) shows an income certificate with the amount region absent from the degraded visible page. The reference is explicitly unreadable; A/C keep null evidence and the bundle requests a clearer upload, rather than inferring a missing document or inventing a value. Zero CER here means agreement with the visible surviving text, not recovery of erased content.

## Breakdowns, timing and limits

The [breakdowns](verification/s10/assets/breakdowns.json) report every route by applicant, class, layout, layout familiarity and degradation. Known layouts have 39 pages /18 sources /three groups; the reserved close layout variant has 13 /six /one. A/B/C character edits are 3/21/44 on known layouts and 0/1/6 on reserved. Exact fields are 120/123, 115/123, 118/123 on known and 41/41, 38/41, 40/41 on reserved. The held-out layout result cannot establish unseen-template generalization from one synthetic applicant.

Clean, low-contrast, rotation and erased-amount strata have 24/12/12/4 pages. A's three character edits occur on rotation; B's 22 also occur there; C has 24 on low contrast and 26 on rotation. Fixed six-label macro-F1 is preserved even in strata missing classes: the income-only erased-amount stratum has macro-F1 1/6 despite all four income predictions being correct. Consult class support rather than comparing such strata as if they had identical label coverage. Every page is synthetic; no real-data stratum or confidence interval is estimated. Repeated variants and bundles are not independent observations.

On the recorded Intel Core i7-12650H /Linux /CPython 3.12.3 environment, the fresh driver's total wall time was 327.128 seconds for all variants and scoring. A/B/C sequential page-batch times were 24.075/21.940/27.428 seconds; OCR counters 18.756/14.245/14.523 seconds, preprocessing 0/1.590/6.634 seconds. Page batches include decoding/PDF rendering, quality, saving originals/stages, DIP, OCR and keyword/field/checklist work; model loading/final JSON writes are excluded. Learned A reuses A OCR, and cached bundle-rule times are not upload latency. [Full timing rows](verification/s10/assets/tables.md#recorded-timing) and [environment metadata](verification/s10/assets/run/run.json) retain the scopes. Caching, load and scheduling were uncontrolled, so differences do not support speed superiority. Replay durations are not fresh OCR measurements.

Limitations remain: 24 test sources/four groups, related templates and one font, five unsupported-content kinds, no real documents, no final multipage/blur/cropping/ambiguous-date/unit-conflict/corrupt-input benchmark, and clean rather than rotated bundle inventories. Those behaviors have development regressions, not final performance estimates. Generator-derived references were source-reviewed by the same agent, with no independent human annotation reliability estimate. Strong readiness agreement on these constructed cases does not prove semantic extraction correctness or authenticity. No model/config/normalization/runtime fix was made after test consumption. S11 must preserve these results while packaging fresh-environment reproduction.
